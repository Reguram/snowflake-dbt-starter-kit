- ⚠️ **1 null CENSUS_BLOCK_GROUP in Patterns** — row exists with null CBG. Should be excluded in staging.
- ⚠️ **106 null RAW_VISIT_COUNT rows in Patterns** — filtered out or imputed downstream.
- ⚠️ **All B-series columns nullable** — no NOT NULL constraints enforced; margin-of-error columns (`m` suffix) often contain -666666666 as a sentinel for "N/A" in ACS encoding. Verify and handle.
- ⚠️ **VARIANT columns require LATERAL FLATTEN** — 7 VARIANT columns in `2019_CBG_PATTERNS` (VISITOR_HOME_CBGS, VISITOR_WORK_CBGS, TOP_BRANDS, POPULARITY_BY_HOUR, POPULARITY_BY_DAY, etc.) need explicit flattening for analysis.
- ⚠️ **Wide tables** — B25 has 100s of columns covering housing sub-categories; B01 has 161 columns. Consider selective projection in staging.
- ⚠️ **2020_CBG_GEOMETRY table missing** — 2019 has `2019_CBG_GEOMETRY` (~2.5 GB) but 2020 only has `2020_CBG_GEOMETRY_WKT`. The heavy geometry table is not present for 2020.
- ⚠️ **No formal PK constraints** — `CENSUS_BLOCK_GROUP` is a natural PK in every table but no database-level constraint enforces it.
- ℹ️ **Typo in metadata column name** — `FIELD_LEVELl_9` (double lowercase L) in `2019_METADATA_CBG_FIELD_DESCRIPTIONS`. Same typo likely exists in 2020 version. Quote carefully.
